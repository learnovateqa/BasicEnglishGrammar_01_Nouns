    ★ The three helper rules

    Capital · Plural · Owner · Position

    1. A special name gets a capital letter. 2. More than one → -s, -es, -ies, -ves, or a new word. 3. Who owns it? → 's or my / mine. 4. Before the verb → I, he, she · after the verb → me, him, her.
